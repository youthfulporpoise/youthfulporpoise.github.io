These are a few pieces I have conjured up from time to time, years old (except the last).
The lot of them are too many to simply lay everything here, and so I have culled them.
They are better put here I think, better than some nook of a drive somewhere.


## My Time, Father

I write under the bane of these wretched times, and in seek of him, Father, I leave, for
I cannot more let suffer this midsommar coming into mere bereave, thus to yonder beyond
unfathomed I veer, Father, that I might call upon this certain seer. (Edition 1)

## A Thousand Kisses

A thousand kisses and a thousand more \
And a thousand and a hundred still \
Then a thousand and a thousand more \
Until we come undone in rapturous thrill


## Chants of the Pimp

That which becomes will cease to be; \
To breathe is but misery. \
To this fate are all doomed to be; \
To turn is all but pearls and ivory.


## Thinking of the Machine

from the quotidian mundanities — or so declaims the distressed. These curios of

## Kneel Not

Peel my skin. \
Shatter my bones.

Strip my hair. \
Gouge my eyes.

Rend my heart \
*Faceless God*!

Quash your boredom; \
I bequeath my woe.

Batter my face. \
Sunder me thence.

A thousand times \
I kneel not to you.
