The image featured in the home page is of an *ophan*, an inexact illustration of its
description from Ezekiel’s vision of [God’s
Merkabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merkabah_mysticism) in the
[Tanakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_Bible), reconstructed with Gemini upon
references from such and such drawings, to evade issues of rights. The ophanim constitute
the chariot-throne, and so the context. More about the ophanim
[here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophanim).

The three images in a row are the goetic sigils of Bifrons, Paimon, and Gäap,
respectively, from Crowley’s *The Book of the Goetia of Solomon the King*. As for the
context, they are presented for the nature/abilities of the spirits they represent,
according to the book.
