This is my blog-ish website, except, more than a blog, it is more of an appendix of what
I do. *What is blood unlike all things red, if it does not congeal nor smells of iron?*
Anything never shown is never seen. What do I stand to gain by keeping to myself all
those thoughts and ruminations that reflect who I really am, how unsightly or unsavoury
it may be? These things are undeniably me, they make me, isn’t it?  And if I conceal
myself, if I always hide behind a mask, won’t I gather everything that finds an abode in
the mask but not me?  And over everything else, such a life, isn’t that a wasted life,
isn’t that a life simply not *lived*?
