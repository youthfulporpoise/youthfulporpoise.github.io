So tell me my dear so-called God, \
Why do you create sin and punish me for it?

Tell me why do you create so beautiful \
These things that sting me when I touch it?

You give me this voice that you never hear \
And a depth of heart that drowns me.

Why do you throw me into this abrupt existence, \
With no foreboding, no warning, nor admonition?

They speak that in you I find peace, and there seems \
Never anything more peaceful than never being born at all.
