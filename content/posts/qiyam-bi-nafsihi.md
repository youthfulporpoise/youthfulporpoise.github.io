It is an attribute of the Islamic God *Allah*. It roughly means “self-subsistence” or
“self-existence.” It is a fundamental attribute that makes God *Al-Qayyūm*. The
implications of this attribute can be surmised as so.

  1. God is entirely independent of everything.
  2. Everything entirely is dependent on God.

That is, God sustains himself, and the Creations. These statements imply a favourable
conclusion. Let us look at the following arguments.

  3. Everything is created by God.
  4. By (1), (2), and (3), God and the Creations cannot share any similarities at all.
  5. By (4), God is transcendent, and therefore, not immanent.
  6. By (5), the essence of God cannot be ever conceived with worldly logics.

For (4): the argument arises from the consequence that if is God is self-subsistent, then
*everything about God* is self-subsistent. Therefore, the Creations cannot have *a part
of God*, which, if not held, will mean that the Creations is *not entirely* dependent on
God, that some of the Creations are self-subsistent. However the following arguments pose
a contradiction.

  7. Existence is unique; there are no different types of existence.
  8. By (1), (2), and (7), both God and the Creations exist.
  9. By (8), God and the Creations are similar, in that they both exist.
