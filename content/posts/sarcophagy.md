There’s a lot of meat in a single shawarma skewer. That’s a lot of chickens killed. We
all love chicken, and for many of us it happens to be their favourite kind of meat. Have
you ever thought of how many chickens are killed worldwide? It’s nearly an ungodly [202
million](https://ourworldindata.org/how-many-animals-get-slaughtered-every-day) every
single fucking day. That’s 1,40,000 killed each minute. Fucking hell, have you ever
thought about that?

See, that’s the thing. When we are enjoying our chicken, we don’t give it a consideration
about how it died and who killed it. My point is not the enormity of that number, that’s
just the harsh truth, no, my point is that: would the ones that so happily eat chicken
kill one?  Pick a chicken with your hands, snap or crush or cut its neck, and watch it as
it lay dying, beating its legs, bleeding out. Those three posh girls, would they? Perhaps
not.  And that’s the hypocrisy. You would play the poor innocent person who wouldn’t hurt
a thing but would be delighted to eat it if it arrived before you on your table. In fact,
I know someone like that, someone who detests the very sight of a chicken being killed and
yet, would you know, it’s his favourite kind of meat.

There’s a far general observation that can be made from this. The fact of how we so
conveniently blind ourselves to hard realities to exempt ourselves from guilt. How we
shut one eye so that we can empathize piecewise to those we want, not to those we don’t.

Every single one of us has done it at some point in our life. Most of us are taught to do
it, and most of us never unlearn it.
