Her eyes dance carnally with mine and \
Her heart concedes, inviting lies splayed. \
I *enter* and inside see and caress \
Countless mars and wheals and wounds: \
The vandals guilty the masters of her birth, \
A childhood oblated to the Diety of Labour.
