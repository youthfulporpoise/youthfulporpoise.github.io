We all know the things **AI** can do. It’s a tool, isn’t it? Yeah, you think so? Well,
you’d be wrong by a mile. But also right. Now I’m not going to involve myself in the
dumbfoolery about whether it’s conscious or not or if it has feelings or any other shit
in that vein (whether it bears responsbility for its decisions, oooooh, now that’s an
interesting question).  **AI** is nothing more than a next-word prediction system, a very
good one. That’s all.  It mimics thought; it doesn’t think, not at all. It can’t reason.
It’s a statistical megalithic black box that’s being fed the complete inheritance of
human history, the bequest of all our knowledge, and it consumes a lot of water (a lot, I
mean, a lot lot) and it vomits what we might *probably* be looking for. And it’s not bad
at it either.  This is not about “**AI** bad, boycott **AI**,” but the normalization of a
pernicious element that we generally lie blind towards. To begin talking about that,
we’ve some proverbial emptying of the cup to do, so to say. For starters, the point of
life isn’t comfort. Yes, that’s where the rich fucks get it wrong. Let me convince you.

Ever heard of the [experience machine](https://w.wiki/5Dfk)? It goes like this. You have
a choice between two options. First one’s that if you choose you’ll be plugged into a
machine where you’ll receive raw pleasure and comfort all the time perpetually. The
machine knows how to exploit the biology of your brain to inject pleasure. You can choose
the second, which is to go on living your normal everyday life. And if you’re concerned
about the machine going haywire and killing you, oh look, the point just flew over your
head.

If pleasure and comfort is the only good, then the first choice is a no-brainer right?
But does something tell you not to do it? Has it become clear to you what it is?  Well
I’ll tell what it is. It’s because we want to do shit, not just simply have the feel-good
of having done shit. So point taken.  The ultimate point of life isn’t comfort: it’s a
twist on the antithesis of [hedonism](https://w.wiki/38f2) by
[Nozick](https://w.wiki/RPvd) that “pleasure isn’t the only good.”

Now you wonder how exactly does **AI** play into that argument. If you’ve a keen eye you
might’ve noticed that **AI** does have something to do with the wanting-to-do-shit part.
If you did, I’m proud of you, you’re getting the hang of it. Now there’s a grey area
between **AI** doing the shit and **AI** helping in doing the shit. It’s a nuance.  And
we’re on our own here. If you’re someone who uses **AI** as a “tool” for drafting an entire
apology letter?  Well, fuck you. That’s supposed to be a person communicating with
another person, and you’ve ruined it for both of you. So fuck you twice. But yes I
understand you. It’s easy to get carried away in there. I feel you, I feel you. I’m
taking one fuck-you back.

If you’re the one who does the shit, you leave your mark on the shit.  If it’s **AI**,
the mark is just **AI**’s, not yours. You shouldn’t offload your entire “personality” to
**AI**.  But that’s precisely what everyone’s doing. And that’s precisely why we’re kind
of totally fucked.
